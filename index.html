What is LoopLab?

A small independent set of three playable browser challenges, made to measure whether people enjoy returning and sharing. This is not an online leaderboard: scores are kept only on your device, and any shared target is user-provided.

Privacy: No account is needed. There are no ads or payments. Scores stay on your device. Optional analytics is off until you choose Allow. When enabled, game starts, completions, replays, sharing actions and theme answers are sent to PostHog with a random browser identifier stored for up to 90 days. This allows us to measure return visits. No names, email, full URLs or recordings are sent by the game. PostHog receives network information such as your IP address when processing requests. Declining does not affect play. Stopping analytics deletes the local identifier and prevents future uploads; it does not erase events already received.

Research disclosure: Saying you might buy a cosmetic option is a survey answer, not a purchase, deposit or promise.
